# Curio - Termite Reference Slice

## Role

The termite Immersion is a reference implementation for the learning loop.

It is not the identity of Curio and should not dominate the future catalog.

## Main question

Why can air circulate in some termite mounds without a fan and without central control?

## Learning goals

The learner should understand:

P1. Differences in heating/temperature can help drive air movement.

P2. Structure, geometry and channels help shape or guide the airflow.

P3. No central controller is required for the circulation.

Optional nuance:
- daily heating/cooling cycle
- gas exchange
- mechanisms vary among species

## Important misconceptions

Do not accept as the main explanation:
- termites actively fanning the nest in this example
- a simple one-way chimney operating the same way all day
- ventilation automatically meaning constant temperature
- queen/leader control of ventilation

## Current learning flow

1. Choose topic
2. Explain what the learner will understand
3. Why air exchange matters
4. Temperature/heating differences
5. Structure and channels
6. No central engineer
7. Day/night cycle
8. Three-point summary
9. Scientific caveat
10. Explain in own words
11. AI feedback
12. Connection to self-organization
13. Finish

The previous pre-learning hypothesis screen was removed because the first user had not yet been taught enough to form a useful hypothesis.

## Classification

### understood
P1 + P2 + P3, no critical misconception.

### partial
Two required ideas, no critical misconception.

### review
Critical misconception or insufficient causal model.

### unable
Too short, corrupt, low-confidence or invalid evaluation.

Unable must not reduce learner progress.

## Example strong answer

Different parts heat and cool differently, which helps move air. The structure and channels guide that movement, and no termite or leader has to centrally control it.

## Scientific framing

The product must avoid universal claims across termite species.

The familiar phrase natural air conditioner is useful as a hook but too simple as the scientific explanation.

## Source direction

For production content, keep exact bibliographic source records in the content database.

## Design lesson learned

Literal termite/mound art was visually confusing.

Use abstract visuals for:
- airflow
- temperature differences
- circulation
- day/night change

This slice should be reused as a structural learning pattern, not copied as the template for every topic.
